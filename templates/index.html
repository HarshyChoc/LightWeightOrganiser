Welcome to your thought canvas

1. Creating Tasks

Double-click anywhere on the canvas to create a new task box

2. Moving Tasks

Drag the edge of a card to move it. Type in the middle to edit it.

3. Connecting Tasks

Click Connect on a parent card, then Connect on another card. Ctrl-click also works.

4. Add detail or remove a card

Use Subtask to make a smaller step. Delete removes a card and its connections; Undo brings it back.
